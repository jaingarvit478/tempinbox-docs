# Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** February 1, 2026

TempInbox uses minimal cookies to operate the service.

## Types of cookies

- **Essential cookies:** keep your temporary inbox session active so you can view received emails.
- **Security cookies:** support abuse prevention and rate limiting.

## Third‑party services

Some deployments may use third‑party services such as bot protection or analytics. These services may set their own cookies.

## Your choices

You can delete cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may prevent the service from working properly.
