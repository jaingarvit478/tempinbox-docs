---
title: "Data Retention Policy"
description: "Temporary storage and scheduled cleanup; no fixed or permanent retention guarantee."
---

This documentation reflects the [TempInbox Data Retention Policy](https://tempinbox.dev/policies/data-retention), effective February 13, 2026. The linked service policy is the authoritative version.

## Mailbox messages

Messages are temporary. Mailbox content may be soft-deleted first and then permanently removed in scheduled batches after a grace window.

## Attachments

Attachment objects and metadata may be soft-deleted and then permanently removed by scheduled cleanup jobs.

## System and security data

Operational and audit records may be kept for security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting, then deleted based on configured retention windows.

## No long-term guarantee

There is no published fixed retention duration or permanent-storage guarantee. Do not rely on TempInbox for long-term archiving or legally required record retention.

Browser-saved access is separate from server-side storage. Clearing browser data can remove local access but does not itself guarantee immediate deletion of server-side data. See [inbox lifetime](/guides/inbox-lifetime).
