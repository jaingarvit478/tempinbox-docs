# Data Retention Policy

**Effective date:** February 1, 2026

Temp Email is a temporary service. We retain data only as long as needed to operate the service.

## Retention periods

- **Messages and inbox metadata:** retained for a limited time (default 30 days).
- **Deleted inboxes:** stored to prevent re‑use and reduce abuse.
- **Logs:** retained for a short period for security and reliability.

## Deletion

- Automatic cleanup is always enabled and deletes messages after the retention period.
- You can delete messages or delete an inbox at any time to remove data sooner.

## Exceptions

We may retain data longer if required by law or to protect the service from abuse.
