## What should I check before choosing a free service?

Check access, acceptance, readable message content, expiry behavior, and recovery. Price does not answer any of these questions.

A public lookup inbox may let others read messages by name. TempInbox instead uses a signed token stored in your browser; knowing the address alone does not grant read access. Anyone controlling that browser profile can use its inboxes. There are no inbox passwords or cross-device recovery.

Read current retention and privacy terms rather than assuming a provider keeps nothing because it is free or requires no signup. TempInbox message retention is unspecified. Its saved browser access is not a permanent archive or a delivery guarantee. A missing email may be delayed, rejected, or never sent; investigate rather than treating a quick successful test as universal proof.

## When should I switch to a durable address?

Switch before the account or relationship becomes important enough that you would need it back after losing your browser data. Purchases, subscription management, private records, and production administrative access need durable ownership and recovery.

An alias forwarding into a mailbox you keep may suit ongoing address separation. A conventional mailbox is appropriate for correspondence and account recovery. TempInbox does not offer custom domains, sending, or account-based recovery; those needs call for another service with explicitly supported features.
