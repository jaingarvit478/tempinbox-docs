TempInbox verifies a signed mailbox access token saved in your browser. Knowing an email address alone does not grant read access. Someone who controls your browser profile or obtains its access token may still be able to read the associated inbox.

## What temporary email protects

For accepted short-term signups, using a disposable address keeps your main email out of that signup database. It can separate receiving workflows and reduce unwanted mail reaching your main inbox.

It does not hide your IP address, cookies, browser identifiers, payment details, or other information you submit. It does not provide anonymity or claim end-to-end encryption.

## Shared browsers and access tokens

Saved access can outlast a tab. Use a browser profile you control and treat its tokens as access credentials. Do not share them or paste them into external tools. TempInbox has no inbox-password feature or supported cross-device recovery.

Clearing site data can remove local access, but does not itself guarantee immediate deletion of server-side messages. Service handling follows the privacy, security, and retention policies.

## Which address should I choose?

| Need | Appropriate approach |
| --- | --- |
| Short-term accepted signup | Temporary inbox, if loss of access is acceptable |
| Ongoing account while hiding the main address | An accepted alias forwarding to a durable mailbox |
| Confidential correspondence or recovery-critical account | A durable authenticated mailbox with suitable protections |

The receiving site's rules determine whether disposable addresses are accepted. Temporary email does not replace account authentication or durable recovery.
